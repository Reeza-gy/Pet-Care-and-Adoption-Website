Pet care

Pet Care Guidance

A happy, healthy pet depends on good daily habits. These general tips cover the essentials of caring for a dog or cat, whether you are a first-time owner or preparing to welcome a new companion.

Everyday Care Essentials

Simple routines make a big difference to your pet's comfort, health and happiness.

🥣

Feeding and Fresh Water

Feed a good-quality diet suited to your pet's species, age and size. Follow the feeding guide on the packaging, and ask your veterinarian if you are unsure how much to give.

Keep to regular mealtimes and avoid sharing human food, as some everyday foods can be harmful to dogs and cats.

Clean, fresh water should be available at all times. Wash bowls daily and refill them often, especially in hot weather.

🎾

Daily Exercise and Play

Dogs need daily walks and time to sniff, explore and play. The right amount depends on their age, breed and energy level.

Cats benefit from short play sessions with wand toys, balls and climbing spaces that let them stretch, chase and pounce.

Play also builds trust between you and your pet. Rotate toys to keep things interesting and supervise play with anything new.

🛁

Grooming and Hygiene

Regular brushing removes loose hair, prevents tangles and gives you a chance to check your pet's skin, ears and paws.

Keep bedding, litter trays and feeding areas clean. Scoop litter trays daily and wash bedding regularly.

Introduce nail trims, baths and tooth brushing gradually, using praise and treats so grooming becomes a calm routine.

🩺

Veterinary Care and Vaccinations

Register with a local veterinary practice soon after adoption and book a general health check.

Keep up to date with vaccinations, parasite prevention and routine check-ups, following your veterinarian's recommendations.

If your pet seems unwell, stops eating or behaves unusually, contact a qualified veterinarian promptly rather than relying on online advice.

ℹ️

This page offers general guidance only. It is not a substitute for professional advice from a qualified veterinarian, who can recommend the right care for your individual pet.

Before You Adopt

Use this checklist to make sure you are ready to give a pet a safe and happy home.

  • I have time every day for feeding, exercise, play and companionship.
  • I can afford food, veterinary care, vaccinations, insurance and supplies.
  • My home is safe and suitable, and my landlord allows pets if I am renting.
  • Everyone in my household agrees to welcome a new pet.
  • I have a plan for who will care for my pet when I am away.
  • I am ready to commit for my pet's whole life, which may be 10 to 20 years.
