About us

About Paws and Homes

Paws and Homes is a community-based animal-welfare organisation that rescues, cares for and rehomes dogs and cats. Our volunteers, foster carers and partner veterinarians work together to give every animal the chance of a better life.

Our Mission

Our mission is to rescue, care for and rehome dogs and cats in need, while helping every adopter feel confident and prepared to give their pet a safe, healthy and loving life.

Our Values

These four values guide every decision we make, from the first day an animal arrives to long after they have gone home.

❤️

Compassion

We treat every animal with kindness and patience, whatever their history, age or personality.

🏡

Responsible Adoption

We match pets and people carefully, so every adoption has the best possible chance of lasting a lifetime.

🌱

Lifelong Pet Care

Our support continues after adoption, with friendly guidance on feeding, training, health and wellbeing.

🤝

Community Support

We work with volunteers, foster carers, veterinarians and local schools to promote responsible pet ownership.

Our Adoption Process

Our simple three-step process helps us find the right home for every pet.

  1. Browse Available Pets

    Explore our adoptable dogs and cats, read their profiles and think about which personality would suit your home and lifestyle.

  2. Send an Adoption Enquiry

    Complete our enquiry form to tell us about yourself, your home and the pet you are interested in meeting.

  3. Arrange a Meeting and Suitability Review

    We invite you to meet the pet and talk through their needs. A short suitability review helps us confirm the match before a decision is made.

Responsible Pet Ownership

Owning a pet means meeting their physical and emotional needs every day, for the whole of their life. Responsible owners plan ahead, ask for advice when they need it and treat their pet as a valued member of the family.

We are always happy to help adopters build good habits from the very first day.

  • Provide a balanced diet, fresh water and a safe, comfortable place to rest.
  • Arrange regular veterinary check-ups and keep vaccinations up to date.
  • Make sure your pet wears identification and is microchipped.
  • Use kind, reward-based training and give plenty of exercise and play.
  • Plan for your pet's care when you are away from home.
