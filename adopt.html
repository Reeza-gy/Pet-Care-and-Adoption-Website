How We Review Adoption Enquiries

Sending an enquiry does not reserve a pet or guarantee adoption. Every enquiry is read by a member of our adoption team, who will contact you to learn more about your home, routine and experience with animals.

Where appropriate, we arrange a meeting with the pet and a short suitability review before any decision is made. This careful process helps each animal settle into a home where they will be safe and happy for life.
